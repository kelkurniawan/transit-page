## Factors That Affect Shipping Costs

### 1. Weight and Volume

This is the primary cost driver. Freight services generally calculate charges based on:

- **Actual weight:** The physical weight of the cargo in kilograms
- **Volumetric weight:** Calculated from the dimensions of the cargo (L x W x H)

Whichever is greater between actual weight and volumetric weight is what's used to calculate the rate. This matters especially for goods that are light but take up a lot of space.

### 2. Type of Goods

The nature of your cargo affects both handling and pricing:

- **General goods:** Standard rate — electronics, textiles, office equipment
- **Industrial goods:** May require special handling — raw materials, machine components
- **Fragile goods:** Need extra packaging and careful handling
- **High-value goods:** May require additional security measures

### 3. Service Type

Delivery speed is directly tied to cost:

- **Regular (1-2 business days):** The most economical rate, suited to routine shipments
- **Express/same-day:** Premium rate, ideal for urgent needs
- **Long-term contract:** Usually comes with special, more competitive rates

### 4. Pickup and Delivery Location

The further a pickup or delivery point is from the main route, the more likely you'll see an additional charge. Easily accessible areas typically get standard rates, while harder-to-reach areas may carry a surcharge — this applies on both the Jakarta side and the Bandung side.

### 5. Shipping Frequency

Customers with high shipping volumes or regular frequency typically get more competitive rates. Monthly or annual shipping contracts are often more cost-effective than paying per shipment.

## Tips for Getting the Best Shipping Rates

### Negotiate a Long-Term Contract

If your business needs regular Jakarta-Bandung shipping, consider signing a long-term contract. Freight companies typically offer significant discounts for committed volume or frequency.

### Consolidate Your Shipments

Instead of sending small amounts of cargo repeatedly, gather your goods and ship them in a single batch. This makes better use of truck space and lowers your cost per unit.

### Choose the Right Schedule

Shipping on weekdays and outside peak hours is generally more efficient. Avoid last-minute shipments that require express service unless it's genuinely urgent.

### Compare Multiple Providers

Don't settle for the first freight service you come across. Request quotes from at least 2-3 providers and compare not just price, but also service quality, on-time performance, and cargo safety.

### Make Sure Your Packaging Is Efficient

Efficient packaging reduces volumetric weight and protects your goods from damage. Investing in the right packaging can lower shipping costs and help you avoid damage claims.
