## Three Service Options, One Right Decision

When you contact a freight service for Jakarta-Bandung shipping, you'll usually be offered a few different service options. The most common: **regular, express, and contract service**.

Most people jump straight to asking about price without understanding the fundamental differences between these three services — and end up choosing something less than optimal. This article will help you make the right call.

## Regular Service: The Workhorse for Planned Shipments

Regular service is the standard shipping option — available every business day, the most economical rate, and suited to most business needs.

### How It Works

You book a shipment, your logistics partner schedules a pickup, and your goods are collected and delivered within 1-2 business days. There's no guarantee of a specific hour, but the day estimate is usually accurate.

### Best For

- Raw material shipments already scheduled well in advance
- Periodic restocking to a distributor
- Goods without a tight deadline
- Businesses whose shipping volume isn't yet large enough to warrant a contract

### Advantages

- The most affordable rate of the three options
- Flexible — book anytime with no commitment
- A familiar, easy-to-understand process

### Limitations

- No guarantee of a specific arrival time (e.g., "guaranteed before 10 AM")
- During peak season, fleet capacity can fill up and schedules can shift
- Not as efficient as contract service for large, routine volumes

## Express Service: A Solution for Urgent Situations

Express service is built for shipments that can't wait. Goods are picked up and delivered with top priority — typically same-day delivery or next-day morning.

### How It Works

Pickup happens in the morning (before a set cutoff, usually 10:00 AM), the truck departs as soon as it's loaded, and goods arrive at their destination that same afternoon or evening. For next-day delivery, departure can happen at night so goods arrive early the next morning.

### Best For

- Urgent components or raw materials needed immediately to keep production running
- Replacement shipments for goods that arrived damaged or were sent incorrectly
- Goods with a tight contract deadline
- Unforeseeable force-majeure situations

### Advantages

- The fastest option — goods arrive the same day or the next morning
- Priority access to fleet and drivers
- Well-suited to genuine emergencies

### Limitations

- Rates run 30-50% higher than regular service
- Depends on fleet availability — not always available at every hour
- Pickup has a cutoff time (you can't book an afternoon pickup for same-day delivery)

### When You Shouldn't Rely on Express

Express service is best used for genuinely urgent situations. If you find yourself using express routinely because of poor planning, your logistics costs will balloon unnecessarily. Take a look at whether there's a planning pattern you could fix instead.

## Contract Service: The Best Choice for Established Businesses

Contract service is the solution for businesses with routine shipping needs and a substantial volume. It's not just a "discount" — it's a deeper logistics partnership.

### How It Works

You and your logistics partner agree on:

- **Minimum volume or frequency** per period (for example: at least 10 shipments per month, or at least 5 tons per month)
- **A planned shipping schedule**
- **A fixed rate** (unchanged for the duration of the contract)
- **An SLA (Service Level Agreement)** — on-time standards, claims procedures, and so on
- **Periodic reports** matching your administrative needs

### Best For

- Factories with routine raw material or finished goods shipping needs
- Distributors restocking a Bandung warehouse on a regular basis
- Companies that want to lock in logistics costs against rate fluctuations
- Businesses shipping large volumes (>2 tons per shipment) consistently

### Advantages

- **More competitive rates** — typically 10-20% lower than the regular rate
- **Guaranteed fleet capacity** — no worrying about not getting a truck
- **More personalized service** — drivers already familiar with your procedures
- **Simpler administration** — one monthly invoice instead of per-shipment billing
- **A stronger business relationship** — your logistics partner understands your specific needs better

### What to Check Before Signing a Contract

- Make sure you can actually meet the agreed minimum volume or frequency
- Read the clauses on what happens if you don't hit the minimum volume carefully
- Make sure the SLA on offer matches your operational needs
- Ask for a trial period if you haven't worked with this logistics partner before

## Quick Comparison

| Aspect | Regular | Express | Contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery time | 1-2 business days | Same day / next day | As agreed in the schedule |
| Rate | Standard | Premium (+30-50%) | Competitive (-10-20%) |
| Commitment | None | None | Minimum volume/frequency |
| Administration | Per transaction | Per transaction | Monthly / periodic |
| Best for | General shipments | Emergencies | Businesses with routine volume |

## How to Choose the Right One

**Use regular if:**
- You're still trying out a new freight service and aren't sure about its consistency yet
- Your volume isn't yet large enough for a contract
- You're not shipping every week

**Use express if:**
- You have a genuinely urgent situation that truly can't wait
- Your goods carry high value and delays would be very costly

**Use contract if:**
- You ship routinely (at least 2-4 times a month)
- Your volume is consistent (not too variable)
- You want predictable logistics costs
- You've used that partner's regular service before and were satisfied
