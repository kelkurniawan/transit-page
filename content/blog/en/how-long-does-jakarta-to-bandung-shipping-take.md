## What Affects Shipping Time

### 1. Pickup Time

When your cargo is picked up plays a big role in when it departs. Generally speaking:

- **Morning pickup (before 10:00):** Allows same-day departure, so cargo can arrive the next day on regular service
- **Midday pickup (10:00–14:00):** Still departs the same day, but may arrive the following day
- **Afternoon pickup (after 14:00):** Usually can't depart until the next day

For same-day express service, pickup needs to happen before a set cutoff time (typically 09:00–10:00).

### 2. Traffic Conditions

The Jakarta-Bandung route via the Cipularang Toll Road is known for unpredictable traffic. A few known hotspots:

- **Bekasi/Cikarang toll entrance:** Often congested during the morning rush
- **Cipularang Toll Road (especially around Km 70–90):** Can get busy during holiday seasons or weekends
- **Pasteur toll gate in Bandung:** Long queues during peak hours

Experienced logistics partners factor this into their departure planning.

### 3. Number and Location of Delivery Points

If your shipment covers multiple points — say, three different warehouses — total delivery time will be longer due to loading and unloading at each stop.

### 4. Cargo Condition and Loading Process

Goods that are easy to load — standard boxes, neatly stacked pallets — move through the process faster. Irregularly shaped, oversized cargo, or goods requiring special strapping take longer to load.

### 5. Weather

Heavy rain, particularly around the Purwakarta and Cipularang areas which are prone to fog, can slow travel for safety reasons. This can't always be avoided, but a good logistics partner will keep you informed when it happens.

## Estimated Time by Service Type

### Regular Service (Most Popular)

Regular service works well for shipments without extreme urgency — planned raw material deliveries, routine warehouse restocking, or standard product distribution.

**Timeline:**
- **Day 1:** Order confirmation and pickup scheduling
- **Day 1 or Day 2 morning:** Pickup from your location
- **Day 2 or Day 3:** Cargo arrives at its Bandung destination

*Note: "business days" excludes Sundays and national holidays.*

### Same-Day Express Service

For urgent needs — a component your factory needs right away, or cargo that absolutely must be in Bandung today.

**Timeline:**
- **Morning:** Pickup happens in the morning (before 10:00)
- **Midday–afternoon:** Truck departs and travels via Cipularang
- **Evening:** Cargo arrives at its destination (typically before 18:00–20:00)

### Next Day Service

A middle ground between regular and express — more reliable than regular, more economical than same-day.

**Timeline:**
- **Day 1 (evening):** Pickup and loading
- **Late night or early morning:** Departure (to avoid traffic)
- **Day 2 morning:** Cargo arrives in Bandung before the business day starts

This is ideal for businesses that need goods ready at the start of the next business day.

## How to Make Sure Your Shipment Arrives on Time

### Book a Day Ahead

For regular service, confirm your order a day before your preferred pickup date. This ensures a truck is available and your schedule can be confirmed.

### Have Your Cargo Ready Before the Driver Arrives

When the driver arrives for pickup, your cargo should already be:

- Properly packed
- Clearly labeled with its destination
- Accompanied by documents (delivery notes, packing lists)
- Ready for loading, with your warehouse team on standby

Slow loading on the shipper's side is a common — and often overlooked — cause of delay.

### Communicate Deadlines Clearly

If you have a specific deadline ("cargo MUST be in Bandung before Tuesday at 08:00"), communicate this when booking. Don't assume your logistics partner knows the urgency unless you tell them.

### Choose the Right Service

Don't book regular service if you need your cargo tomorrow. Regular service is estimated at 1-2 business days — meaning it could arrive on day 2, not guaranteed on day 1. If there's urgency, choose express instead.

## What If a Shipment Is Delayed?

While rare, delays can happen due to severe traffic, bad weather, or unexpected incidents on the road. What sets a good logistics partner apart from an average one is **how they handle delays**:

- Proactively informing you as soon as there's any sign of delay
- Providing updated arrival estimates
- Offering alternative solutions when possible
