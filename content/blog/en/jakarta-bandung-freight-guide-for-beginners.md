## First Time Using a Freight Service? Here's What You Need to Know

Many growing business owners feel unsure the first time they need to arrange shipping for large quantities of goods. Sending a personal package through services like JNE or Gojek is familiar territory — but using a cargo freight service for B2B needs is a different game altogether.

This guide is written specifically for you if you're using a professional freight service on the Jakarta-Bandung route for the first time. No unnecessary technical jargon — just practical steps you can put to use right away.

## What's the Difference Between Cargo Freight and a Regular Courier?

Before you start, it's worth understanding this difference so you know what to expect.

**Regular courier services (JNE, J&T, SiCepat, Gojek):**
- Ideal for small packages, under 30-50 kg
- Dropoff or pickup systems, but usually for small volumes
- Priced per kilogram, which gets expensive for large loads
- Not ideal for industrial goods or bulk cargo

**Cargo freight services (like Transit):**
- Ideal for shipments from hundreds of kilograms up to tens of tons
- Pickup directly from your warehouse or factory
- Far more cost-efficient for large volumes
- A truck fleet sized to match your capacity needs
- Genuine door-to-door service

If your business ships more than 100-200 kg per shipment, a cargo freight service is by far the more efficient choice.

## Step One: Work Out Your Shipping Requirements

Before contacting a freight service, have the following information ready:

### Weight and Volume

- **Actual weight** (weigh the goods once packed)
- **Total dimensions** (L × W × H in cm), or dimensions per unit
- **Volumetric weight** = (L × W × H) / 4,000 — use whichever figure is greater

### Frequency

- Is this a one-time shipment or a recurring one?
- If recurring, how often? (daily, weekly, monthly)

### Type of Goods

- What kind of goods are they? (electronics, textiles, food ingredients, machine components, etc.)
- Is anything fragile or in need of special handling?

### Location

- The exact pickup address (including RT/RW, building or area name if relevant)
- The delivery address at your Bandung or Jakarta destination, depending on which direction you're shipping

This information is what you'll need to get an accurate price quote.

## How to Contact a Freight Service

Most B2B freight services are reached via WhatsApp or phone — not through a website or app the way a typical marketplace works.

When you first get in touch, share:

1. "I'd like to ship goods from [your location] to [destination in Bandung/Jakarta]"
2. Your estimated weight and/or volume
3. The type of goods
4. When you need it shipped (is there a deadline?)
5. Whether this is a one-time shipment or something you plan to do regularly

Their team will respond with clarifying questions or go straight to a price quote.

## Understanding the Price Quote

When you receive a quote, check:

**Whether the price already includes:**
- Pickup costs (collection from your location)?
- Toll and fuel costs?
- Delivery to the destination?

**Whether there are extra charges for:**
- Hard-to-reach locations?
- Upper floors with no elevator (for building deliveries)?
- Loading/unloading by the driver?

A transparent freight service will lay all of this out upfront. If anything's unclear, don't hesitate to ask.

## Your First Shipment, Step by Step

### Step 1: Confirm the Order

Once you've agreed on price, confirm:
- The pickup date
- The pickup time window (e.g. "between 09:00-11:00")
- The estimated arrival time

### Step 2: Prepare Your Documents

At minimum, you'll need:
- **A delivery note (surat jalan)** — this can be a form your logistics partner provides, or one you create yourself. At minimum it should include: sender's name, sender's address, recipient's name, destination address, description of goods, and quantity.
- **Package labels** — attached to every box/unit

### Step 3: Prepare Your Goods

- Goods should already be packed and ready to load
- Arrange them somewhere the driver can easily access
- Have your team on standby to help with loading

### Step 4: When the Driver Arrives

- Show the driver the goods and documents
- Count the loaded items together
- The driver signs a receipt
- Keep the driver's contact info for updates during transit

### Step 5: Confirm Receipt

- Let the recipient know the estimated arrival time
- Once the goods arrive, the recipient signs a receipt
- Confirm back to the sender that everything checks out

## Frequently Asked Questions from First-Timers

**Q: Is my cargo safe?**
A: A professional logistics partner takes responsibility for your goods while in transit. Make sure you get a receipt that notes the condition of the goods at loading. For very high-value goods, ask about shipping insurance options.

**Q: What if my goods get damaged?**
A: Claims procedures vary by company. Ask about the claims process before you use the service. Document the condition of your goods before shipping (photos) as evidence.

**Q: Can I ship liquids or chemicals?**
A: It depends on the specific goods. Hazardous materials (B3) require special permits. Check with your logistics partner first to see if they can handle your specific type of cargo.

**Q: Can I request an invoice for tax purposes?**
A: A professional logistics partner can provide an official invoice. Ask about this in your first conversation.

## Tips for a Successful First Shipment

**Don't cut your schedule too close.** For your first shipment, build in extra buffer time. This gives you room in case something unexpected comes up on the admin or logistics side.

**Communicate your expectations clearly.** If there's something specific you need (for example, cargo must arrive before a certain time), say so upfront. Don't assume your partner already knows.

**Keep all your documents.** The signed delivery note, proof of payment, and correspondence throughout the process — all of this is useful if any issue comes up later.

**Give feedback after the shipment.** If anything fell short, say so. A good logistics partner will value the feedback and act on it.
