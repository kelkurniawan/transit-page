## Bekasi: An Industrial Hub With Heavy Logistics Demand to Bandung

Bekasi is one of Indonesia's largest industrial regions. Thousands of factories, distribution warehouses, and business centers operate along the Bekasi corridor — from MM2100 in Cibitung, EJIP (East Jakarta Industrial Park), and KIM (Kawasan Industri Menara) in Cikarang, to the Jababeka estate.

Many of these companies ship to Bandung on a regular basis — sending finished goods to Bandung-based distributors, or receiving raw materials from Bandung suppliers. But finding a **freight service from Bekasi to Bandung** that actually does door-to-door pickup from Bekasi's industrial zones isn't always easy.

## Routes from Bekasi to Bandung

There are a few route options from Bekasi to Bandung:

### Route 1 (Most Common): Via the Jakarta-Cikampek + Cipularang Toll Roads

- Bekasi → Jakarta-Cikampek toll road (entering via the Bekasi or Cikarang gate)
- Cikampek → Purwakarta → Padalarang (Cipularang toll road)
- Padalarang → Bandung (Purbaleunyi toll road)

Distance: ±160–180 km (depending on the pickup point in Bekasi)
Normal travel time: 3–4 hours

### Route 2: Via the Cisumdawu Toll Road (Cileunyi-Sumedang-Dawuan) — A New Alternative

The newly completed Cisumdawu toll road opens up an alternative route from Bekasi/Karawang straight into East Bandung (Cileunyi). This can be more efficient for deliveries to East Bandung areas (Ujung Berung, Arcamanik, Rancasari).

Our team picks the best route based on traffic conditions at the time of departure.
